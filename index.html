Guild Wars 2 Rotation Simulator

Under the hood

The simulator runs the rotation in order and tracks skills, cooldowns, resources, boons, conditions, and damage.

Fixed mode uses average values and returns the same result each time. RNG mode runs 500 trials with random weapon strength and supported procs to show the likely range of DPS.

Results are single-target estimates. Use them to compare builds and rotations, not as exact benchmarks.
