About this tool

This is a standalone Mesmer simulator. Select gear, upgrades, consumables, weapons, specializations, traits, and slot skills; attributes are calculated from those selections and passed directly into the rotation simulation.

  • Core Mesmer, Chronomancer, Mirage, Virtuoso, and Troubadour profession mechanics.
  • Cooldown-aware icon palette: icons show remaining cooldown and the simulator waits until a queued skill becomes available.
  • Shift+click instant skills to cast during the preceding animation. Ctrl+click casts to interrupt them at a chosen time.
  • Expected-value strike and condition damage with clones, phantasms, shatters, ambushes, blades, notes, and supported trait interactions.
